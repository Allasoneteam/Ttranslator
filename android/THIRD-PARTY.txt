Ttranslator for Android — programs of other projects inside the APK, their licences and their sources

Ttranslator itself: MIT licence, Allasoneteam and Ttranslator authors (LICENSE.txt).
Source: https://github.com/Allasoneteam/Ttranslator (the tag of this release).

1. llama.cpp, release b11435 — the engine (llama-server and its libraries)
   MIT licence, The ggml authors (the text below).
   The official build for Android of the release: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/releases/tag/b11435
   (llama-b11435-bin-android-arm64.tar.gz). Source: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/tree/b11435

2. sherpa-onnx, release v1.13.7 — reading aloud (libsherpa-onnx-c-api.so, used by libsherpa-onnx-offline-tts.so)
   Apache License 2.0, Xiaomi Corporation and the sherpa-onnx authors (the text below).
   The official libraries for Android of the release: https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/tag/v1.13.7
   (sherpa-onnx-v1.13.7-android.tar.bz2). Source: https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/tree/v1.13.7
   (commit 917bed95c8e5c7c18aa4d69fea42e9ef8ef0a60e). The small program libsherpa-onnx-offline-tts.so is
   Ttranslator's own (android/native/tts-cli.c, MIT).

3. espeak-ng — inside libsherpa-onnx-c-api.so (sherpa-onnx turns text into sounds with it)
   GNU General Public License, version 3 or later (the text below).
   The exact source sherpa-onnx v1.13.7 builds it from (cmake/espeak-ng-for-piper.cmake of that release):
   https://github.com/csukuangfj/espeak-ng/archive/ed530aa113046142eb5115cf2fc9157854d0ffe1.zip
   (SHA-256 e4e262cbe34f7fe21f91f1ba3397f2728e1f30eafbae7853f2b753a9ed13f0dd); the build instructions are in the
   sherpa-onnx source above (build-android-arm64-v8a.sh). If a link stops working, ask in the issues of
   https://github.com/Allasoneteam/Ttranslator and we will send you the source.

4. piper-phonemize — inside libsherpa-onnx-c-api.so
   MIT licence, Michael Hansen (the text below).
   Source (as built by sherpa-onnx v1.13.7, cmake/piper-phonemize.cmake):
   https://github.com/csukuangfj/piper-phonemize/archive/f3ff95afc03640bc1399e113e83361192a2fafb4.zip

5. ONNX Runtime — libonnxruntime.so, the build that comes with the sherpa-onnx release above
   MIT licence, Microsoft Corporation (the text below). https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime

6. The Go standard library — compiled into the launcher (libttranslator.so)
   BSD-style licence, The Go Authors (the text below). https://go.dev/LICENSE

Not in the APK: the models (Gemma 4: Google, Apache 2.0) and the voices (Piper, packed by sherpa-onnx) are downloaded
when you choose them, each with its own licence (shown in the menu of models).
